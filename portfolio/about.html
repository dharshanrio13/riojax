My Journey

Personal & Cybersecurity Journey

From curiosity to commitment — the path that shaped my passion for technology and security.

⚡ The Spark
Discovering Technology
My fascination with computers began early — taking apart old machines, exploring operating systems, and asking "how does this actually work?" That curiosity never left.
🐧 Linux Awakening
Switching to Linux
Installing Linux Mint changed everything. The command line stopped being intimidating and became a playground. I started automating tasks, customizing my environment, and understanding systems at a deeper level.
🛡️ Cybersecurity Calling
Choosing B.Tech Cyber Security
The decision to pursue Cyber Security wasn't random — it was the natural convergence of my interest in how systems work and how they can be protected (or broken). Every CTF, every lab, every concept learned fuels this path.
🚀 Building & Sharing
Rio Jax is Born
I started building real projects — from automation scripts to full websites — and sharing my work under the alias Rio Jax. Every project is a step toward becoming a well-rounded tech professional.
